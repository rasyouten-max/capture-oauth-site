Privacy Policy
隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-11-01 · Contact / 联系方式：rasyouten@gmail.com

1. Scope and operator

Capture (OAuth application name: Intelligence Monitor PROD clasp) is a private, owner-operated research and document automation project. This policy covers the public informational website and the Google OAuth-enabled automation. It is not a general-purpose service for public account registration.

2. Google data accessed and why

  • Google account email: identify the authorized operator and bind account-level access.
  • Google Sheets: read, create, update, and organize research tables, configuration records, processing status, and results.
  • Google Drive: read, create, update, and organize research source materials, supporting files, reports, and evidence archives.
  • Script execution and management: operate authorized Apps Script functions, maintenance actions, deployment/version management, and configured triggers.
  • Email-sending scope: send notifications using the authorized Google account only if configured by the owner. This scope is not a general-purpose email marketing tool.
  • External requests and editor UI: retrieve public source material or call owner-configured services, and display approved interfaces within Google editors.

These are broad Google permissions. They should be granted only to an account operator who trusts and controls this automation. Available permissions do not mean that every operation is executed in each Run.

3. Processing, storage, and sharing

Research records and generated files may be stored in the owner's Google Drive, Sheets, and related Apps Script storage. Credentials used by the associated automation may be stored in access-controlled Google Script Properties and encrypted GitHub Actions Secrets. The public website itself contains no OAuth tokens or private research data.

When the owner enables external source or model providers, selected research queries, public-source content, and processing inputs may be transmitted to those providers to perform the requested research task. Data processing by Google, GitHub, and enabled external providers is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. Capture does not sell Google user data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Website visitors

This is a static site. It does not intentionally deploy its own analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, or contact forms. Its hosting platform may process ordinary access logs, such as IP address and request metadata, under that platform's policies.

5. Retention and control

Research files and operational records are maintained under the owner's account and applicable service retention settings. The owner may remove files, disable an integration, rotate credentials, or revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access through that grant but does not automatically delete files previously created by the owner.

6. Security and updates

Access is intended to be restricted to the authorized operator. Secret values are not designed to be published in this site or the public source repository. This policy may be updated when the actual system's capabilities or data handling change; the date above identifies the current published text. For privacy-related requests, contact rasyouten@gmail.com.

中文说明

适用范围

捕影（Google OAuth应用名称：Intelligence Monitor PROD clasp）是由所有者自行管理的个人研究与文档自动化工程，并非面向公众开放注册的在线服务。本政策同时涵盖本公开说明网站与获得Google授权的自动化流程。

可能访问的数据及用途

  • Google账户邮箱：核对授权操作者身份。
  • Google Sheets：读取、创建和更新研究数据表、配置、阶段状态及结果。
  • Google Drive：读取、创建和管理研究材料、报告及证据档案。
  • Apps Script：执行受控函数、维护版本和部署、管理相关触发器。
  • 邮件发送：仅在所有者配置相应功能时发送工作通知，不作为营销邮件工具。
  • 外部请求及编辑器界面：采集公开资料、调用所有者配置的服务，并显示Google编辑器交互界面。

Google权限范围较广，只有对该自动化工程具有管理权并理解其用途的操作者才应授权；持有权限不意味着每次运行都使用所有权限。

处理、存储和共享

研究数据和生成文件可能存放在所有者的Google Drive、Sheets或Apps Script存储中。运行凭据可能保存在受访问控制的Script Properties和加密GitHub Actions Secrets中。公开网页不保存OAuth令牌或私有研究材料。若所有者启用外部搜索或模型服务，特定研究查询、公开来源内容和处理输入可能提交给相关服务完成任务，并受相应平台的隐私条款约束。捕影不出售Google用户数据，也不用于定向广告。

网站访问与数据保留

本站为静态页面，不主动设置统计追踪、广告像素、Cookie或资料收集表单；托管方可能依其政策保留常规访问日志。研究资料由所有者按账户和服务设置管理，可删除文件、停用连接或通过Google账户连接页面撤销授权。撤销授权不自动删除已经生成的文件。

安全及联系

系统设计为仅由获得授权的操作者访问。密钥不应进入公开网页或公共仓库。若数据处理方式发生变化，将更新本政策。有关隐私问题请联系 rasyouten@gmail.com。
