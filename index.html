Why does Capture request Google access?

Google authorization supports the owner's Apps Script workflow. Depending on the enabled functions, it may read or modify research files in Google Drive and Sheets, display editor interfaces, manage script deployments and triggers, make authorized external requests, use the account's email identity, and send owner-configured notifications.

为什么需要Google授权？

Google授权用于运行由所有者控制的Apps Script工作流。根据启用的功能，系统可能读取或修改Google Drive及Sheets中的研究文件、显示编辑器界面、维护脚本部署与触发器、发起授权的外部请求、使用账户邮箱核对身份，以及发送所有者配置的通知。

This public website is informational only. / 本公开网站仅供说明。

It has no sign-in form, credential entry, tracking script, or direct access to your Google data. Authorization occurs through Google's own consent screen, not here.
